# Mahjong Together house rules

These are casual house rules, not an official tournament variant.

- The set has 136 tiles: dots, bamboo, and characters numbered 1–9; four winds; and three dragons, with four copies of each tile type.
- East, South, West, and North take turns in that order. The dealer starts with 14 tiles and discards first; other turns draw, then discard.
- Win with four sets and one pair, counting declared melds. A set is a suited sequence, a pung, or a kong. A fully concealed hand may instead win with seven distinct pairs.
- Any player may claim a discard to win or make a pung/exposed kong. Only the next player may chow. Claims resolve by win, then kong/pung, then chow; nearer seats win equal-priority ties.
- Kongs draw a replacement from the back of the wall. Added kongs may be robbed for a win; concealed and discard-claimed kongs may not.
- There are no points, minimum values, ready declarations, furiten, flowers, jokers, multiple winners, or dealer retention. A hand ends with one winner or a draw.

The interface will contain the complete playable explanation when implemented.
