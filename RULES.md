# Mahjong Together house rules

These are casual house rules, not an official tournament variant.

- The set has 136 tiles: dots, bamboo, and characters numbered 1–9; four winds; and three dragons, with four copies of each tile type.
- East, South, West, and North take turns in that order. The dealer is marked on the table, starts with 14 tiles, and discards first; other turns draw, then discard. The dealer rotates to the next seat after every hand, including when the host returns to the lobby between hands.
- Win with four sets and one pair, counting declared melds. A set is a suited sequence, a pung, or a kong. A fully concealed hand may instead win with seven distinct pairs.
- Any player may claim a discard to win or make a pung/exposed kong. Only the next player may chow. Claims resolve by win, then kong/pung, then chow; nearer seats win equal-priority ties.
- Kongs draw a replacement from the back of the wall. Added kongs may be robbed for a win; concealed and discard-claimed kongs may not.
- There are no points, minimum values, ready declarations, furiten, flowers, jokers, multiple winners, or dealer retention. A hand ends with one winner or a draw.
- Select a tile and use **Discard selected** on your turn. Available responses appear after a discard; only the next seat can Chow. The end-hand dialog shows the winner, source, complete winning combination, rematch controls, and the other players’ sorted hands.
- A turn has 30 seconds and a claim or added-kong-robbery window has 10 seconds. An unanswered claim passes. If a player disconnects during a hand, a bot takes over after a random 1–5 second delay; returning before that action keeps the same available decision and remaining timer.
- A room is removed as soon as its final human player leaves. After a hand ends, the host has three minutes to start another hand before the room closes; auto-play starts the next hand after 15 seconds unless the host turns it off.
- The newest tab is the active controller for a guest. A replaced tab offers **Take control here**, which safely makes it the current controller again. A server restart ends in-memory rooms and sessions; the game returns an expired room to the home screen so a new table can be created.
